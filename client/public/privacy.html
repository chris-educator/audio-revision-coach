Privacy Policy

Audio Revision Coach · AppStax · Last updated: 7 July 2026

Audio Revision Coach (revise.appstax.ai) provides flashcard decks and listen-aloud revision scripts for Australian secondary students.

What you submit

Revision topic choices, optional custom topics, optional Ask Ed the Assistant chat, and account sign-in for EdStack credits.

What we store

  • Generated decks & scripts — returned to your browser for that session; not stored in an AppStax database.
  • Revision topics — sent to Anthropic and/or Google for that request only; not stored on AppStax servers.
  • Listen mode — uses your browser text-to-speech locally; audio is not sent to AppStax.
  • Ask Ed the Assistant — chat messages are sent to Google for that request only; we do not store chat history on AppStax servers.
  • Account — email and credits via EdStack billing when you sign in.

AI processing

Deck and script generation are sent to Anthropic (primary) and/or Google (Ask Ed the Assistant help, and fallback where configured). Providers process data under their own terms. We do not use your content to train AppStax models.
