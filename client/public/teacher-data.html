Student Data Handling

Audio Revision Coach · AppStax · For schools and parents · Last updated: 7 July 2026

Revision topics and generated decks/scripts are processed per request and are not retained on AppStax servers. Listen mode uses browser text-to-speech locally. Contact apps@appstax.ai for contractual questions.
